# Rebuild Novara's page around proof, not glow

Novara's landing page should become a **proof-first booking page**. The core is a multi-step instant-price widget, a prominent tap-to-call path backed by the AI receptionist, a separate Airbnb/STR turnover band, and dated, sourced reviews. AI should show up as a few small UI moments that explain what is happening (a price building line by line, a call transcript card, a dispatch timeline), not as a decorative layer. High-converting home-service pages share a small set of parts: a ZIP or address-first hero with a price anchor, a bedrooms/bathrooms/frequency/extras quote form with a running summary, review aggregates, a named guarantee, a "what's included" checklist, an FAQ, and a sticky mobile Call + Get Price bar. The 2025-2026 "agentic" visual toolkit (voice orbs, streamed agent steps, integration beams, bento grids, aurora gradients, dark glow) is real, but its decorative half now reads as "AI slop". Experiments also show that labelling a product "AI" lowers purchase intent, and the effect is strongest for high-risk purchases. Letting strangers into your home qualifies. The code audit also found three issues to fix before any redesign work:

- **The page still claims "Google Guaranteed" and "Backed by Google's $2,000 guarantee."** Google consolidated that badge into "Google Verified" and dropped the money-back guarantee in October 2025.
- **The hero H1 is wrapped in a `BlurFade` that starts at `opacity: 0`.** This is a known way to delay LCP.
- **The stack is pinned to the last React 18 / Tailwind v3 line of HeroUI.** Most new registry code assumes React 19 / Tailwind v4.

A note on evidence: the research subagents could not fetch most live competitor sites or library documentation, because those hosts were outside the session's network allowlist. The findings below rest on web-search snippets, secondary write-ups, npm registry data (which was reachable), and Novara's own repo. Items marked "verify" need a browser check before anyone builds on them.

## Cleaning sites split into five hero types, and Novara should combine two

Home-service heroes fall into five recognizable types. **Franchises lead with ZIP lookup plus a free-estimate form.** Molly Maid pairs "Find a Molly Maid Near Me" with a quote form promised to take "under 2 minutes" ([Molly Maid](https://www.mollymaid.com/)), and Merry Maids leads with "Request your free estimate today!" ([Merry Maids](https://merrymaids.com/)). **Marketplaces lead with a price:** Homeaglow's "$19 first cleaning" ([Homeaglow](https://www.homeaglow.com/hub/content/homeaglow-cost)) and TIDY's "starting price $55, no subscription or contracts" ([TIDY](https://www.tidy.com/comparisons/homeaglow)). **Lawn marketplaces turn an address into an exact price** using satellite measurement ([LawnStarter](https://www.lawnstarter.com/blog/lawnstarter/how-does-lawnstarter-instant-quote-work/)). **HVAC and plumbing pair "Book Online 24/7" with a phone CTA** ([ppc.io](https://ppc.io/hvac-landing-page-examples)). **MaidPro makes its process the headline**: "With Our 49-Point Checklist, You'll LOVE Your Clean!" ([MaidPro](https://www.maidpro.com/)).

Novara's current hero is the franchise type, with a discount line. The strongest upgrade combines a ZIP/home-details entry with a visible live "from $X" price, plus a tap-to-call number. That merges the marketplace price hook with the trades dual CTA. Two limits apply. First, the price must come with clear terms. Homeaglow's $19 offer, tied to a membership with booking fees ([The Everygirl](https://theeverygirl.com/homeaglow-cleaning-services-review/)), has produced a body of competitor attack content ([A-1 Clean](https://a1clean.net/exposing-the-truth-behind-homeaglows-19-cleaning-service/)). Novara's existing "$99 first clean" and "15% / 25% off" lines need the same plain-terms treatment. Second, keep the copy simple. Unbounce's 2024 benchmark of 41,000 pages found **5th–7th grade copy converted at 11.1% versus 5.3% for professional-level copy** ([DMN](https://dmn.ca/unbounces-2024-conversion-benchmark-report-proves-that-attention-spans-are-declining-and-so-are-conversion-rates/)).

The booking widget itself is close to an industry standard. BookingKoala and Launch27 forms use bedrooms, bathrooms, optional square footage, service type, frequency with discounts, priced extras (oven, fridge, cabinets, baseboards, blinds), and a running summary panel ([Complete Cleaners on BookingKoala](https://completecleaners.bookingkoala.com/booknow); [ConvertLabs](https://blog.convertlabs.io/cleaning-business-scheduling-software)). Multi-step forms beat single-step forms with the same fields by about 21% on average in relayed Unbounce data ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/landing-page-statistics-2026-conversion-data-points)). Single-step still often wins below five fields ([IvyForms](https://ivyforms.com/blog/multi-step-forms-single-step-forms/)). The right shape is a step one of only one or two fields (ZIP, home vs. Airbnb), then details, frequency, extras, time slot, and contact, with a sticky price summary on desktop and a bottom sheet on mobile.

Phone matters as much as the form. Invoca's 2026 home-services report finds **45% of lead calls convert on the call, but only 52% of home-services calls are answered by a person** ([Invoca](https://www.invoca.com/reports/the-invoca-home-services-lead-conversion-benchmarks-report-2026)). Novara answers every call with an AI receptionist, which closes exactly the gap that makes phone leads leak. Agencies consistently name a hidden or non-tappable phone number as the top mistake on home-service sites ([Haskell Digital](https://www.haskelldigitalservices.com/blog/website-mistakes-home-service-businesses)). Home-service landing pages convert at a median of roughly **6.7%**, with a top quartile above 17.6% ([Barham Marketing](https://barhammarketing.com/27-home-services-conversion-rate-statistics-for-2026/)). A competing benchmark gives 8.5% ([CorePPC](https://coreppc.com/blog/landing-page-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2026/)), so treat these as ballpark figures.

## Trust now depends on recency, a real guarantee, and correct badges

Reviews are the biggest trust lever, and the bar keeps rising. BrightLocal's 2026 survey found **31% of consumers only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or higher, up from 17%**, and **74% look for reviews from the last three months** ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)). Novara's testimonial marquee shows star icons, a name, and a location, but no date, no source, and no service type. That fails the recency test. Two changes fix this: an aggregate rating chip in the hero ("4.9 on Google, N reviews", once verified) and dated review cards labelled "Airbnb host" or "Homeowner".

The badge problem is urgent and is a correctness issue, not a styling one. From October 20, 2025, Google merged "Google Guaranteed", "Google Screened", and "License Verified by Google" into a single **"Google Verified" badge and discontinued the money-back guarantee** ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-to-update-local-services-ads-policies-in-july-479653); [JumpFly](https://www.jumpfly.com/blog/google-local-service-ads-launch-consolidated-google-verified-badge/)). `src/views/Index.tsx` still shows "Google Guaranteed & Fully Insured" in the hero pill, a "Google Guaranteed" trust card reading "Backed by Google's $2,000 guarantee", and "Google Guaranteed cleaners" in the SEO description. A `GoogleGuaranteedBadge.tsx` component also exists. Replace all of these with "Google Verified" only if Novara holds current LSA verification. Whether the badge graphic may appear on a business's own website is publicly unresolved, and a practitioner forum raises trademark and suspension risk ([Local Search Forum](https://localsearchforum.com/threads/can-local-service-businesses-display-the-google-guaranteed-verified-badge-on-their-site.63421/)). Verify with Google before shipping the logo; a text mention is safer.

Novara's own guarantee is a stronger asset than it gets credit for. MaidPro re-cleans if notified within 24 hours ([MaidPro](https://www.maidpro.com/)), and Molly Maid within the next business day ([Neighborly](https://www.neighborly.com/about/molly-maid)). **Novara's 48-hour window beats both** and deserves its own component, not a slot in a six-card grid. Proprietary process is the other proven trust device. MaidPro has a dedicated 49-point checklist page and lets customers drop tasks ([MaidPro checklist](https://www.maidpro.com/checklist)). Novara already has a `checklists` component directory, so a room-by-room "what's included" table comparing Standard, Deep, Move-out, and Turnover is cheap to build and directly competitive.

STR hosts need different proof from homeowners. Turno, the reference product for hosts, sells calendar sync with Airbnb/Vrbo/Booking.com, cleanings auto-generated "for every reservation and guest checkout", schedules that update when guests extend or cancel, and photo checklists ([Turno](https://turno.com/features/auto-scheduling/); [Turno photo checklists](https://turno.com/features/photo-checklists/)). An STR band that shows a mock post-clean photo report and a dispatch timeline will persuade hosts more than any before/after slider.

## The credible agentic pattern is live proof, and the decorative pattern is cliché

The 2025-2026 AI-native toolkit has two halves. The **functional half** shows the agent working. ElevenLabs puts "Voice chat" and "Create an AI agent" up front ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/)). Hume offers "Talk to Hume" ([Hume](https://www.hume.ai/)). Agent UIs render tool calls as cards with loading and error states and reasoning as collapsible blocks ([LangChain](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/javascript/langchain/streaming/frontend)), and guidance stresses explicit states: thinking, executing, waiting, completed, error ([inference.sh](https://inference.sh/blog/ux-agent-ux-patterns)). Home-service AI vendors converge on one move: let the prospect hear a call. Sameday uses "Hear Sameday take a call" ([Sameday](https://sameday.ai/)), Hatch pairs "Talk to Hatch Voice AI" with "Book a Demo" ([Hatch](https://www.usehatchapp.com/product/gpt-live-1-voice-ai)), and Avoca hosts real calls with PII scrubbed out ([Avoca](https://help.avoca.ai/demos)). No consumer cleaning brand was found marketing its own AI receptionist on its landing page, which is either white space or deliberate restraint.

The **decorative half** includes aurora glow, indigo-to-purple gradients, Inter, three rounded cards in a row, and bento grids. It has become the recognizable fingerprint of AI-generated design ([925 Studios](https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells); [Fountain Institute](https://pages.thefountaininstitute.com/posts/7-tells-that-a-ui-is-ai-generated)), and designers were already mocking bento for its ubiquity by 2025 ([Visual Movements](https://visual-movements.pages.dev/movements/bento/)). This is awkward for Novara, whose brand is **purple #6810FE, set in Inter, with a particles canvas, a radial blur halo, and a `text-gradient` headline**. That is almost exactly the cliché list. The fix is not a rebrand. Use #6810FE as a confident flat accent on a warm off-white base, drop the radial halo and particles, let Plus Jakarta Sans carry headlines, and use real photography of named cleaners. The leading AI brands are moving the same way: Anthropic's 2025 campaign was praised as "warm, human, considered… in a sea of corporate blues and neon gradients" ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/creative-inspiration/advertising/claudes-new-ad-is-a-refreshing-take-on-ai-branding)). Warm serifs are themselves becoming a convention ([AI Directory](https://aidirectory.com/news/ai-companies-turn-to-serif-fonts-to-look-more-human)), so don't chase that either.

Messaging research points the same way. Washington State University experiments with more than 1,000 adults found that **adding "artificial intelligence" to otherwise identical descriptions lowered purchase intent, most strongly for high-risk products** ([WSU](https://news.wsu.edu/press-release/2024/07/30/using-the-term-artificial-intelligence-in-product-descriptions-reduces-purchase-intentions/)). Pew found 50% of US adults more concerned than excited about AI ([MarketingProfs/Pew](https://www.marketingprofs.com/charts/2025/53727/Americans-more-concerned-than-excited-about-ai-study-pew)). Disclosure and a human fallback flip the effect. Five9's 2026 research found **55% trust AI to resolve an issue when a human option exists versus 26% without one** ([CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/clear-path-to-live-agent-boosts-trust-ai-customer-service/825835/)), and about 80% want to know when they are talking to AI ([CX Today](https://cxtoday.com/why-ai-disclosure-could-make-or-break-customer-trust)). Most of these surveys are vendor-commissioned. Still, the conclusion holds: headline the outcomes ("answered 24/7", "exact price in 60 seconds", "a cleaner confirmed before you hang up"), name the AI in supporting copy, and put "a real person is one tap away" next to every AI touchpoint.

The full component inventory comes down to three groups. **Keep:** shadcn primitives, HeroUI 2.8, and Magic UI `marquee`, `number-ticker`, `blur-fade` (below the fold), `shimmer-button`, `shine-border`/`border-beam` (one instance each), and `magic-card`. **Retire from the hero:** `particles`, the radial blur halo, and `text-gradient` on the H1. **Add:** `@number-flow/react` (MIT, React 18/19, [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@number-flow/react)) for the live price; Magic UI `animated-beam` and `animated-list` via the already-registered `@magicui` registry; an orb or waveform for the call demo; and `react-compare-slider` (MIT, React ≥16.8, [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/react-compare-slider)) only if real before/after photos exist. Skip the decorative backgrounds (Aceternity aurora, React Bits ogl/three scenes, globes) and bento grids for this page. They add weight and push the page toward the cliché.

## Stack constraints that decide what is safe to install

React 18 and Tailwind v3 are the binding constraints. **HeroUI v3 (3.2.7) requires `tailwindcss >=4` and `react >=19`.** The installed 2.8.10 is the last v3-compatible line, so do not upgrade it as part of this work ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@heroui/react)). Most registries now emit Tailwind v4 code by default. Existing v3 shadcn projects keep working ([shadcn](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/tailwind-v4)), but pasted v4 code needs utilities like `bg-linear-*` and `@theme` variables translated back to v3 by hand. coss/Origin and Tailark are described as v4. Launch UI keeps a Tailwind 3 branch ([GitHub](https://github.com/launch-ui/launch-ui)).

The agentic libraries carry the most risk. **Vercel AI Elements will be deprecated at the end of November 2026 in favor of a shadcn/ui-hosted version**, per its npm README ([npm: ai-elements](https://www.npmjs.com/package/ai-elements)), and a third-party listing says it needs React 19 and Tailwind v4. A marketing page needs neither the AI SDK nor a live chat, so recreate the Chain of Thought look with motion and shadcn cards. ElevenLabs UI (MIT; Orb, Waveform, Bar Visualizer, Voice Button) is the obvious source for the call-demo visual ([shadcn.io](https://shadcn.io/awesome/item/elevenlabs-ui)), but its React 18 / Tailwind v3 support and any three.js dependency are unconfirmed. React Bits is MIT plus Commons Clause: production use is fine, repackaging is not ([GitHub](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/discussions/1101)). GSAP uses its own no-charge license, not MIT ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/gsap)).

Performance rules follow from the same sources. A hero that starts fully transparent delays LCP until the fade completes, and canvas particle loops compete with the LCP element for the main thread ([savvy.co.il](https://savvy.co.il/en/blog/wordpress-speed/hero-animation-lcp-optimization/)). **Novara's hero does both today:** `BlurFade` animates the H1 from `opacity: 0` with a 6px blur, and `Particles` runs behind it. Render the H1 and quote card statically. Load any canvas or audio-visual component through `next/dynamic({ ssr: false })`, mount it only when it scrolls into view, mark it `aria-hidden`, and freeze it under `prefers-reduced-motion` using motion's `useReducedMotion()` ([shadcnspace](https://shadcnspace.com/blog/how-to-build-animated-hero-section)). Two housekeeping items also need attention. `components.json` has `rsc: false`, so newly added animated components need `"use client"` added by hand. framer-motion ^12 and motion ^13 are both installed, against a current v14.9.1 ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/motion)); consolidating them avoids shipping two motion runtimes.

Verify these before building:

- Live hero layouts of Molly Maid, Merry Maids, Homeaglow, Handy, TIDY, and MaidThis.
- Exact current component names and Tailwind v3 compatibility of Magic UI `animated-beam`/`animated-list`, the ElevenLabs UI Orb/Waveform, the coss stepper, and Animate UI.
- Whether shadcn-hosted AI Elements is live.
- Novara's actual LSA verification status, and Google's policy on showing the badge on a business's own site.
- Real review counts and stat values to replace any unverifiable numbers in `TRUST_STATS`.
- Web.dev and Next.js primary documentation for the LCP and lazy-loading guidance (only secondary sources were reachable).

No cleaning-specific A/B data on instant price versus "get a quote", on AI-forward versus AI-quiet pages, or on warm versus tech aesthetics was found. Treat those choices as hypotheses to test.

## Conclusion

Novara's advantage is that it can show things most cleaning brands cannot. Its AI answers the call that competitors miss half the time, prices the clean exactly, and dispatches a named cleaner. The research shows these work as proof, not as branding: hearing a booking call or watching a confirmation timeline earns trust that the word "AI" costs. The redesign is therefore mostly subtraction and correction. Take out the glow, particles, and outdated Google claims. Stop fading the headline. Then add three functional moments (live price, call demo, dispatch timeline) and a separate host path.

The first tests to run are price-anchored hero versus ZIP-only, the call-demo section on versus off, and a dated review grid versus the marquee. The lack of cleaning-specific data means Novara's own analytics will settle these questions faster than more desk research.
