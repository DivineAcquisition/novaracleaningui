Novara Cleaning ('we,' 'us,' or 'our') is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our cleaning services, whether as a customer or independent contractor.

By engaging our services or accepting our independent contractor agreement, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
1.1 Customer Information

We collect the following information from customers:

Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, billing address, and service location address

Property Information: Property type, size, access codes, gate codes, lockbox codes, special instructions, and pet information

Payment Information: Credit card numbers, bank account information, billing history, and transaction records

Service Information: Booking dates, service preferences, cleaning checklists, special requests, and service history

Photos and Documentation: Before-and-after photos of cleaned areas for quality assurance and documentation purposes

Communications: Email correspondence, text messages, phone call records, and customer feedback

Device and Usage Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and website usage patterns

1.2 Independent Contractor Information

We collect the following information from independent contractors:

Personal Information: Legal name, date of birth, Social Security Number or Tax ID, driver's license, address, phone number, and email

Professional Information: Work history, references, skills, certifications, and availability schedule

Financial Information: Bank account details for direct deposit, earnings records, platform fee deductions, and tax documentation

Insurance Information: Proof of liability insurance, policy numbers, coverage amounts, and expiration dates

Location Data: GPS location tracking during service hours to verify job completion, arrival times, and service area coverage

Performance Data: Job completion rates, customer ratings, quality assessments, timeliness metrics, and probationary period evaluations

Photos and Documentation: Before-and-after photos submitted for all cleaning jobs as required by contractor agreement

Communications: All communications with operations team, customers (as permitted), and support staff

Disciplinary Records: Job abandonment incidents, suspension records, and termination information

Schedule, coordinate, and provide cleaning services

2.2 Contractor Information Usage

We use contractor information to:

Maintain evidence for contract enforcement and legal proceedings

3. GPS LOCATION TRACKING

GPS location tracking is a critical component of our service verification and contractor management system.

3.1 Contractor GPS Tracking

Independent contractors agree to GPS location tracking during scheduled service hours. We collect and use GPS data to:

Verify arrival at customer locations and job completion

Document time spent at service locations

Optimize routing and service area coverage

Investigate service disputes or complaints

Protect against false claims and chargeback fraud

Ensure contractor accountability and service quality

3.2 GPS Data Retention

GPS location data is retained for a minimum of four years to comply with evidence retention requirements and to protect against legal claims, chargebacks, and contract disputes.

4. PHOTO DOCUMENTATION

Before-and-after photos are mandatory for all cleaning services to ensure quality assurance, verify service completion, and protect against fraudulent claims.

4.1 Photo Collection and Usage

Photos are used for:

Quality control and contractor performance evaluation

Documentation of pre-existing conditions and completed work

Protection against false damage claims and service disputes

Evidence in chargeback disputes and legal proceedings

Training and best practice demonstrations

Marketing materials (only with explicit customer consent)

4.2 Photo Storage and Retention

All photos are timestamped and stored securely in our data management system. Photos are retained for a minimum of four years to comply with evidence retention requirements and legal defense obligations.
